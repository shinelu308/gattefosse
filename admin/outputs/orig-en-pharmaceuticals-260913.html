When lipids go solid: process strategies to enhance bioavailability in oral dosage forms

  • Bioavailability enhancement

07 Jul 2026

Lipid excipients are known to improve the bioavailability of APIs by increasing their solubility and permeability. Generally liquid at room temperature, they are primarily formulated in liquid forms. Solid oral dosage forms (OSDs), such as tablets, can also benefit from the advantages of lipid excipients. This webinar highlights various processes for incorporating lipid excipients into tablets or powders intended for oral administration.
